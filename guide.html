Italiano · How to use

A five-minute guide

Flashcards and notes for each lesson.

1 Open it and keep it

Open uam225.github.io/italiano-flashcards once with internet. After that it works offline.

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  • Laptop: bookmark it, or use the install icon in the address bar.

2 Pick a lesson

The lessons are in the sidebar. On a phone, tap the menu button at the top left.

40 Lezione 7
Qualcosa da mangiare 12

The ring shows how much of the lesson you've mastered. The amber number is how many cards to study today.

3 Study

Press Study. For each card:

  1. Read the front and say the answer out loud.
  2. Tap the card to flip it. Tap Ascolta to hear the Italian.
  3. Rate how well you knew it, honestly.
AgainDidn't know it. It comes back in a few cards.
HardGot there, with effort. Back sooner than Good.
GoodKnew it. Back in a few days.
EasyInstant. Back much later.

Cards you know well come back less and less often. Ones you find hard come back soon. Come back each day to clear what's due.

Under Topics & options you can pick topics, choose Italian → English or English → Italian, set how many new cards you get per day, and type your answers instead of saying them.

4 Test yourself

The Test tab gives multiple-choice questions on the topics you've picked. You get a score and a list of what to look at again. Tests don't change your study schedule.

5 Progress and notes

Progress shows your streak, how much you've mastered and how much you remember. Notes has the grammar tables and vocabulary from each lesson.

Good to know

  • Your progress is saved on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Each device and browser keeps its own progress. Pick one to study on.
  • Clearing your browser data resets your progress.
  • No sound from Ascolta? Your device needs an Italian voice. On Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → Italian. iPhones and Macs have one built in.
  • Opening the page from a WhatsApp or email preview won't work. Open the link in Safari or Chrome.
