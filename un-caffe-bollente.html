Lesson notes

Grammar words

Word typeWhat it doesExamples
NounNames a thing, person, place or ideacaffè, birra, finestra, amore
VerbAn action word: what you doabitare, parlare, scrivere
AdjectiveDescribes a nouncaldo, freddo, rosso, italiano
AdverbDescribes an adjective or verbmolto, troppo
  • A noun has a fixed gender. It can only change number (singular to plural).
  • An adjective changes gender and number to match its noun.
  • Adverbs never change: molto calda, molto caldi.

Making nouns plural

Singular ends inUsuallyPluralExample
-afeminine-ela limonata → le limonate

-eeither (each noun fixed)-ilo spumante → gli spumanti

  • Only the last letter changes. The article changes too.
  • In il succo d'arancia, only the juice goes plural: i succhi d'arancia.
  • Add an H to keep a hard sound: succo → succhi, fresco → freschi, bianco → bianchi.
  • More examples: casa → case (house), finestra → finestre (window), porta → porte (door), quaderno → quaderni (exercise book).

At the bar

  • La piadina: a flatbread, filled like a sandwich, eaten hot or cold. More common in the north (Bologna, Milan).
  • Il caffè d'orzo: barley “coffee”, made like an espresso. Orzo = barley.
  • La spremuta d'arancia is freshly squeezed (spremere = to squeeze). Il succo d'arancia is the shop-bought juice.
  • In a café you can drop con: un panino prosciutto e formaggio.
  • In summer Italians drink caffè freddo and granita di caffè, coffee granita often served with cream.
